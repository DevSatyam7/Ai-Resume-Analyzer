Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Is CareersAnalysis free to use?

Yes. This platform is an open student-led project created to assist college peers and freshers in auditing their resumes and preparing for placements without subscriptions.

How accurate is the ATS scoring algorithm?

The engine simulates standard corporate parsing rules (token density, section tags, action-oriented bullet verbs, and skill-to-role semantic alignment) to identify common candidate omissions.

What is the difference between Prep Hub and Topic Explorer?

Prep Hub delivers structured corporate placement blueprints (TCS NQT, Cognizant) and university semester syllabi. Topic Explorer provides fast, on-demand query drills for single subjects and viva prep without uploading a resume.
