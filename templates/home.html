Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Is CareersAnalysis free to use?

Yes. This platform is an open student-led project created to assist college peers and freshers in auditing their resumes without paid subscriptions.

How accurate is the ATS score?

The scoring model inspects semantic relevance, keyword completeness, and action-verb strength against modern corporate ATS criteria, providing actionable percentile scores.

Is my uploaded resume text stored?

Your resume content is securely analyzed in-memory during execution and is neither sold nor retained for model re-training.
